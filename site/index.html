Tomorrow's electricity price in Sweden, forecast hour by hour

Every morning, before Nord Pool's day-ahead auction closes at 12:00, Elmorgon forecasts each hour of the next day for Sweden's four price areas, and checks itself against the obvious guess: the same prices as the day before.

When this page updates

  1. 10:00Elmorgon issues the forecast for tomorrow and rebuilds this page.
  2. 12:00Nord Pool's day-ahead auction for tomorrow closes.
  3. 13:00Nord Pool publishes tomorrow's prices; they are scored here at the next morning's update.

Stockholm time; in winter the morning update runs at 09:00. Spot prices in SEK/kWh, without energy tax, VAT, electricity certificates, surcharges or grid fees.

    Where the information comes from

    Prices. Nord Pool day-ahead prices for the four Swedish price areas, collected from elprisetjustnu.se, which converts them from euros to kronor. Hourly until 30 September 2025, 15-minute since; the forecast is for the hourly average.

    Weather. Temperature, wind, sunshine and cloud cover at two places in each area, from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0).

    Forecast. Elmorgon's own model, retrained every morning on all prices published so far, and saved before the real prices exist. It is not a Nord Pool figure.

    Not included. Energy tax, VAT, electricity certificates, supplier surcharges and grid fees. Prices are rounded to two decimals.
