When this page updates

  1. 10:00Elmorgon issues its forecast for tomorrow and this page is rebuilt.
  2. 12:00Nord Pool's day-ahead auction for tomorrow closes.
  3. 13:00Nord Pool publishes tomorrow's prices. They are shown and scored here at the next morning's update.

Times are Stockholm time; in winter the morning update runs at 09:00. All prices are spot prices in SEK/kWh, without energy tax, VAT, electricity certificates, surcharges or grid fees.

Where the information comes from

Prices
Nord Pool day-ahead prices for the four Swedish price areas, collected from elprisetjustnu.se, which converts them from euros to kronor. The market cleared hourly until 30 September 2025 and in 15-minute intervals since; the forecast is for the hourly average.
Weather
Forecasts of temperature, wind, sunshine and cloud cover at two places in each area, from Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0).
Forecast
Made by Elmorgon's own model, retrained every morning on all prices published so far. It is not a Nord Pool figure, and it is saved before the real prices exist.
Not included
Energy tax, VAT, electricity certificates, supplier surcharges and grid fees. Prices are rounded to two decimals, so totals can differ slightly.

What a better forecast is worth

Each forecast is turned into a plan, and the plan is paid for at the prices that actually cleared, for every day of the backtest in all four zones. Spot price only: grid fees, energy tax and VAT are left out.

How it's built

A daily pipeline, from raw API responses to this page. The same steps run on a laptop, in Dagster, or as a scheduled CI job.

    Python, DuckDB, dbt, Dagster, LightGBM, SciPy and pytest. The page itself is plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript reading one JSON file.
